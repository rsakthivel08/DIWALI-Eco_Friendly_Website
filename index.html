🌿 Green Diwali

Eco Tips DIY for Kids Pollution Pledge

Celebrate light, not waste.

Simple, colorful and kid-friendly ideas to make your Diwali joyful and planet-friendly.

🪔

Easy Eco Tips

Low-smoke options

Use sparklers or eco-friendly fireworks in small amounts and away from congested areas.

Say no to plastic

Choose reusable or paper decorations instead of single-use plastic items.

Smart lights

Use LED lights and timer switches to save electricity. Group lights into zones to switch off when not needed.

Respect quiet hours

Keep noise levels considerate—celebrate with music and songs instead of loud crackers.
